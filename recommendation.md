# Recommendation

**Operational recommendation:** Treat forecast PM2.5 at or above 165 µg/m³ as hazardous: limit outdoor exposure, protect vulnerable groups, and use properly fitted N95/KN95 masks when outdoor travel is unavoidable. Authorities should prioritize current health guidance and prepare school, transport, and hospital responses for consecutive hazardous days.

**Limitations:**
- The submitted forecast uses a pooled ridge model with sensor effects, lagged and rolling PM2.5 features, weather covariates, seasonal terms, and recursive multi-day prediction.
- Recursive forecasts can accumulate error across the 10-day horizon, especially during abrupt pollution events.
- AQI readings are converted using standard PM2.5 AQI breakpoints and missing readings are excluded.

**Reference forecast fallback used?** No.
